# WebRajya Hinglish Style Guide (हिन्दी + English)

This style guide establishes the voice, vocabulary, and spelling standards for WebRajya's website copy, written in natural Roman Hinglish for Indian retail shop owners.

---

## 1. Core Voice & Philosophy
- **Target Audience:** Indian small & medium retail shop owners, store managers, and billing counter cashiers across Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 cities.
- **Tone:** Warm, direct, respectful, and grounded. Always use **"aap / aapka"** — never "tu/tum". Avoid corporate jargon ("Unified SaaS engine", "Enterprise paradigm") and avoid forced memes or slang ("bro", "mast", "chill").
- **Core Feeling:** When a shopkeeper reads this, they should think: *"Yeh mere jaise dukaandaar ke liye bana hai."*
- **Clarity over cleverness:** Short sentences, active verbs, one benefit per line.

---

## 3. What Stays in English (Never Translate)
- **Product Names:** WebRajya, WebRajya Invoice, WebRajya POS, MyGarage.
- **Everyday Technical Terms:** GST, GSTIN, HSN, UPI, QR code, WhatsApp, SMS, PDF, Excel, CSV, KOT, IMEI, Bluetooth, USB, Thermal Printer (58mm / 80mm).
- **Core Retail Verbs/Nouns:** Bill, counter, stock, barcode, scanner, demo, trial, license, software, backup, offline, cloud.
- **Currency Format:** Always use Indian Rupee symbol with comma notation: `₹599`, `₹5,999`, `₹25,999`, `1.8 lakh`.
- **Dates:** Format as `DD/MM/YYYY` or `15 August 2026`.

---

## 4. Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- Use familiar retail concepts: *"Counter par rush ke time zero lag"*, *"Kachcha aur pakka GST bill dono aasaani se nikalein"*, *"Udhaar ka hisaab register ke badle software mein"*.
- State support honestly: *"Video call aur WhatsApp par personal setup madad poore India mein, aur Nagpur mein counter par aakar visit"*.
- Keep numbers and guarantees strict: Never invent fake reviews, fake user counts, or fake percentage savings.
- Use tasteful micro-emojis sparingly (e.g., WhatsApp button, success notification), never in legal or pricing tables.

### Don't:
- Don't use heavy Sanskritized Hindi: Avoid words like *सुलभ, कार्यप्रणाली, विन्यास, अधिभार*.
- Don't use disrespectful or overly casual slang: Avoid *bhai*, *yaar*, *chill maro*, *jugaad*.
- Don't mix roman spellings inconsistently (e.g., using `dukan` on one page and `dukaan` on another).
- Don't hide legal specifics: Keep the detailed legal contracts in formal English, with a plain Hinglish summary at the top.
